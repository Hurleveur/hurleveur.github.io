Alignment

Getting my present self to act for my future self — habits, health, attention, and the systems that hold when willpower doesn't.

Oct 05, 20263 min read#quote

Some of us think about the issue of AI alignment, that it may not protect our interest as it grows.

But are we humans even aligned?
Think about it.
Do we always act in our best interest?
Or are we more irrational creatures?
Yet there are rules that apply to us. I read about those rules, Influence The Psychology of Persuasion and others, I thought about the problem I faced for so long: how to align myself?

Here are the keys of what I use to align my current self with future self, with how I want to think. AI and thinking

I struggle with alignement myself. Everyone does, if they’re honest about it. if you believe you make sense, you don’t is the trap, and it’s the first thing worth sitting with.

Daily realization, On the shortness of time. Time is the one resource you can’t get back, so Powerful power-less sundays and Mindful Daily Schedule are how I keep from wasting it on autopilot.

private, guardians of knowledge — there’s no one standing between you and the internet anymore, that’s a gift and a trap both.

private to zoom out. Les forces de la vie to remember what actually unites people through it. private, because you should have one.

Noise, Take pride in what you do, Use capitalism the right way — capitalism isn’t evil, it’s a system, and systems do what you build them to do. why you should write if you want to think clearly about any of this.

atomic habits and A story is a deal are books that shaped a lot of it. Beauty doesn’t fit anywhere else and I like it that way.

Life structure is the day-to-day, practical version.

What belongs in this room

The ground floor: the body and the day. How I actually run — sleep, food, attention, routine, and the systems that keep a decision made once from being remade every morning. atomic habits, Daily realization, Mindful Daily Schedule, Life structure, Powerful power-less sundays. Owning your own tools rather than renting them is the same instinct one floor down — Linux, why I deleted my social media accounts, Noise.
Not here: what something is for — that is Meaning. What I know about a subject — Library. A tool or a link I recommend — Shared. The AI notes that belong here are the ones about not losing yourself to it (How AI should be used, AI and thinking); AI as something to work on is Work. This room is named for aligning myself with myself — the machine-learning sense of the word is a coincidence.
